What should the recipe detail page look like?

This is the main screen you'll use while cooking. It needs to be readable on a phone propped up on the counter.

A: Stacked (mobile-first)
Chocolate Cake
Desserts & Baking · Serves 8 · p.42
🔖 Saved ✓ Made
A rich, moist chocolate cake with a glossy ganache topping.
Ingredients
□ 2 cups all-purpose flour
□ 1¾ cups granulated sugar
□ ¾ cup cocoa powder
□ 2 eggs, at room temperature
□ 1 cup buttermilk
Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease two 9-inch round cake pans.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, and cocoa.
3. Add eggs, buttermilk, and vanilla. Beat on medium for 2 min.
Everything stacks vertically. Ingredients have checkable checkboxes. Same layout on phone and desktop — just wider text on desktop.

Single Column (Stacked)

Ingredients above instructions, everything stacked. Checkable ingredient boxes. Simple, works great on phone. Desktop gets more whitespace but same flow.
